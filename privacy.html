Privacy Policy – YT Pipeline

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Who uses this app

YT Pipeline is used only by the owner of the YouTube channel "Arise Manga" to manage that channel. It has no other users.

What data is accessed

With the owner's permission (Google OAuth), the app accesses the owner's own YouTube account to:

  • upload videos, titles, descriptions, thumbnails and English subtitles created by the owner;
  • read statistics of the owner's own videos (views, likes, comment counts) and the owner's own channel analytics (watch time, average view percentage, subscribers gained, shares), to see which formats work.

The app does not access, collect or store data about any other person or any viewer.

How data is stored

The OAuth token and video statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared or sent to any third party.

YouTube and Google

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
