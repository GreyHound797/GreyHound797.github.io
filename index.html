YT Pipeline

YT Pipeline is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Arise Manga, on the owner's own computer. It makes the owner's own videos:

  • India news and entertainment news Shorts: short summaries of stories from several named news outlets, with free Wikimedia Commons photos (credited);
  • general-knowledge exam quiz Shorts;
  • animated data videos from World Bank Open Data.

Scripts are drafted with AI and checked against the sources; the voice is text-to-speech. Every video says it is AI-generated and names its sources.

The tool uses the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API to:

  • upload and schedule those videos, with thumbnails and English subtitles, on the owner's own channel;
  • read the statistics and analytics of the owner's own videos to improve future content.

It is not offered to the public and has no other users.
